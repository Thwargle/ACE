## Behavior and compatibility

The reported custom keymap now imports 156 action bindings and 17 explicit
DoNothing overrides. Overrides prevent disabled chords from falling through to
another movement action. Mouse-wheel and keyboard Menu-key bindings import, and
held mouse look works independently of the normal mouse-turning option.

Selection shortcuts follow retail ordering; inventory shortcuts support stack
quantity entry and existing transfer/use paths. Panel shortcuts open the intended
subtab. The scrollable import report distinguishes unsupported controls from
unchanged system/widget contexts. Mapping edits remain a draft until OK is pressed.

Compatibility is not complete: the supplied file has 19 unsupported control
entries and 49 records in nine unchanged native input contexts. Legacy DirectInput
joystick controls, browser Back, and map-camera/Help/plugin-manager actions remain
unsupported. Save As exports supported mappings; retain the original retail file.
See `KEYMAP-PARITY-2026-09-23.md` for the detailed audit and references.
