## Implementation checklist

- **Loading:** asynchronously acquire cooked material parents before destination
  loading; move DAT indexing/cache jobs to dedicated workers; parse layouts off
  thread, construct detached widget trees in bounded batches, and reuse them at
  world reveal. Add timing markers for appearance and HUD preparation.
- **Terrain memory:** filter mips and pack lossless restore data before game-thread
  texture creation; update recreation/mip provider and render audit accounting.
  Keep 1024-square retail blend textures and unchanged GPU pixels/mip chains.
- **Fog:** add `MPC_ACEWorldFog_v1.uasset`; regenerate runtime material parents to
  read the shared collection; preserve local fog masks and old baked-landscape
  compatibility. Include the collection AND regenerated material assets in Git.
- **Collision:** resolve remaining movement across successive contact planes;
  use capsule separation normals at rounded edges; preserve earlier constraints
  when sliding along corners; expand training-hall approach/retreat coverage.
- **Water:** apply the outdoor DAT landing floor to desktop as well as VR;
  reproduce and cover the Holtburg bank, neighboring river block, Rithwic, and
  the reported DC56001F water across stationary/running jumps and two frame rates.
- **Missiles:** require draw amount/time only for bows on the server; distinguish
  stance errors from insufficient draw; respect bow recovery; report failed
  atlatl/thrown sends. Verify actual ammo/contact/consumption in integration tests.
- **VR use:** initialize/rearm the two-hand gesture correctly; use raw tracked
  hands and nearby visible bounds at hand height; retain peace-mode, deliberate
  hold, reach, gaze, use-range, occlusion, and withdrawal safeguards.
- **Settings/UI:** saved independent mouse inversion; desktop scale in 25% steps
  bounded by viewport fit; FPS/frame-time overlay on both desktop and VR; fix
  container-scoped vitals lookup; new rounded enemy health bar without labels or
  numbers and with updates only when its health changes.
- **Launcher/release:** Quit action saves lobby entries; version footer reflects
  the platform build; Quest packaging rejects a displayed/APK version mismatch;
  bump Quest to 60 and Windows to 2026.09.21.66; update public release notes.
