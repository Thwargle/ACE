# AC:Unreal / AC:VR v169

Windows, PC VR, Linux, Quest and the Android preview share version **2026.11.27.169**.

- AC2 ground textures now use detailed cobblestone roads at their original scale. Road shoulders and mixed terrain preserve the resolution of the individual surfaces. Ice, lava and several other ground types have closer matches. Unmatched surfaces retain their original AC1 artwork.
- On startup, players with an existing AC2 import get the updated artwork automatically when the client can locate the matching source DATs. Graphics selections are preserved. If an older custom installation cannot be located, select its portal.dat once in Graphics Options. Future imports remember the folder.
- On Quest, if the original DATs are on your PC, use Install-AC2-Sky.cmd to transfer the refreshed desktop Legions.sky. Then select Import AC2 artwork under Options > VR settings > Graphics.
