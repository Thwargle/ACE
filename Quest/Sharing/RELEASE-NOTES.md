# AC:Unreal / AC:VR v167

Windows, PC VR, native Linux, standalone Quest and the Android preview share version **2026.11.24.167**.

- Item Tools and UCM restock in batches that fit your packs. They account for space in existing coin stacks and redeem trade notes as needed.
- In Item Tools, use **Convert Peas to MMDs** at a pyreal vendor that sells 250,000-pyreal trade notes. The vendor's prices determine how many notes you receive. Leftover pyreals stay in your inventory.
- Open **Plugin Manager** from the main bar on the login screen to update Item Tools to 1.2.0 and UCM to 0.7.1. Official plugins list Thwargle as their developer.
- The Vitae panel uses the retail text and spacing, including the experience needed to recover the next 1%.
- Standalone Quest selects the Vulkan device profile at startup and blends terrain texture detail levels. If v166 hangs on three dots, extract the complete v167 Quest installer and run **Update-Quest.cmd**. This preserves saved logins and game data.
- VR server operators can update their local server files without starting the server. Tracked head and hand poses and physical melee require a server build that supports VR extension discovery.
